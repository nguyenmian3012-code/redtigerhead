# Recruitment privacy baseline

This is a **product baseline**, not a final legal opinion.

The live recruitment form should disclose, in clear language:

- who collects applicant data;
- the recruitment purposes;
- what data is collected;
- whether a field is required or optional;
- who may access the data;
- how long it is retained;
- how an applicant can request access, correction, deletion/withdrawal where applicable;
- a clear consent action when consent is the relevant basis.

The production text should be reviewed against Vietnamese personal data protection requirements before accepting real applications.

Baseline UX decision:
- phone number: required
- National ID / CCCD: optional at initial application
- explicit consent checkbox: required before submission
- privacy notice: available before submission
